Protecting Personal Data Against Spam & Phishing

Published May 2024 • 6 min read • By Nixinbox Security Team

Email remains the primary attack vector for cybercriminals globally. According to industry security studies, over 90% of organizational data breaches originate with a single malicious email link or credential phishing attempt.

How Phishing Campaigns Target Personal Emails

Phishing works by exploiting trust. Attackers mimic logos and tone of voice from popular banks, social networks, or cloud providers. When your real email address is widely distributed on marketing lists, automated bots routinely send credential harvesting templates to your primary mailbox.

Defensive Measures for Online Security

  • Compartmentalization: Isolate your primary email for financial and sensitive logins only.
  • Use Disposable Inboxes for Unknown Platforms: Test new tools and web services with disposable mailboxes like Nixinbox before trusting them with personal details.
  • Inspect Link Destinations: Hover over hyperlinked text to verify destination URLs prior to clicking.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Enforce hardware key or authenticator app protection on critical services.
