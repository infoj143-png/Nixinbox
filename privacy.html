Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2024

1. Overview & Service Purpose

At Nixinbox (nixinbox.vercel.app), protecting your privacy is our core operational principle. As a free, disposable email and temporary inbox utility service, Nixinbox enables users to receive temporary messages without registering personal details, revealing real email addresses, or providing personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Information Collection and Storage

Nixinbox operates with a strict minimal-data approach:

  • No Personal Account Registration: We do not require your real name, identity, phone number, or persistent primary email to generate temporary email addresses.
  • Temporary Inboxes: Temporary email addresses, authorization tokens, and active inbox configurations generated on Nixinbox are stored locally on your device via browser local storage (`localStorage`) so your inbox session remains active while you navigate the site.
  • Automated Data Expiration: Emails sent to disposable addresses are automatically deleted and permanently removed from our backend provider systems after a brief retention window. We do not maintain permanent historical backups of user messages.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners

Nixinbox utilizes standard web technologies, including cookies and log files, to ensure site security, analyze aggregated traffic trends, and deliver relevant advertisements.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Nixinbox or other websites on the internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard web application servers, Nixinbox records general non-identifying server log information. This may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamp, and click counts. This data is purely used for diagnostic maintenance, preventing server abuse, and ensuring system stability.

5. Security

While temporary email messages are processed securely over HTTPS protocols, temporary inboxes are public and disposable by nature. Users should avoid using temporary email addresses for highly sensitive financial credentials, government IDs, or confidential passwords.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page.
