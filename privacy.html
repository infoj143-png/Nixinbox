Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview & Service Purpose

At Nixinbox (nixinbox.vercel.app), protecting user privacy is our foundational operating principle. As a free disposable temporary email service, Nixinbox enables individuals to receive messages without registering personal accounts, providing real contact details, or disclosing personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Data Collection and Retention

Nixinbox operates under a strict minimal-data collection architecture:

  • Zero User Registration: We do not ask for names, addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or primary email details.
  • Local Session Management: Temporary email session keys and active mailbox configurations generated on Nixinbox are saved locally on your client device via browser local storage (`localStorage`) so your inbox session persists while you interact with the site.
  • Automated Data Deletion: Messages received by temporary addresses expire automatically and are permanently deleted from backend server systems. Nixinbox does not maintain permanent archives or long-term message logs.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

Nixinbox uses standard web cookies and log files to ensure platform stability, diagnose security threats, and serve non-intrusive advertisements.

Google AdSense Policies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Nixinbox or other websites on the internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on visits to our site and across the web.
  • Users can manage personalized ad preferences or opt out by visiting Google Ads Settings or reviewing choices on aboutads.info.

4. Technical Server Logs

Standard web server logs record non-identifying technical metadata including IP addresses, browser agent types, referring URLs, date/time timestamps, and access latency. This metadata is strictly utilized for server performance maintenance, DDoS prevention, and rate-limiting security.

5. Security Guidelines

Although incoming email transmissions are protected via HTTPS encryption, temporary inboxes are ephemeral and public by nature. Users are strongly advised against using disposable mail for sensitive banking records, government documentation, or essential permanent account passwords.

6. Inquiries and Contact

For questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly via our Contact Us Page.
